Senior Software Engineer

Techolution, Hyderabad · client: iHeartMedia, US

I work on iHeartMedia's music catalog and ingestion platform. Record labels and distributors deliver DDEX ERN releases with audio. The platform validates, normalizes, enriches, rights-checks and transcodes that content, then publishes tracks, albums and artists to downstream systems.

  • Lead and mentor the engineering team on the platform: drive technical design, review pull requests, plan work with US stakeholders, and coach engineers to own features end to end.
  • Own core stages of a Python, event-driven ingestion pipeline built on FastAPI, Dramatiq actors over RabbitMQ and PostgreSQL, publishing catalog records to Kafka.
  • Re-engineered a complex data migration from several days to about 2 hours with partitioned, parallel Redshift-to-PostgreSQL loads.
  • Cut query latency by 80% by profiling bottlenecks, adding targeted indexes and replacing OFFSET scans with keyset pagination.
  • Designed the PostgreSQL schemas and Alembic migrations for a third-party music metadata platform, plus full and incremental loads and the matching workers for artists, albums, tracks, genres, bios and images.
  • Split heavy workloads into dedicated ECS services and built CloudWatch step-scaling on queue depth, so workers scale out under load and back in when queues drain.
  • Integrated a major label's content-delivery feed end to end: list and download workers, Redis distributed locks, delivery tracking, and New Relic and Slack alerting via a custom-metric Lambda.
  • Shipped rights and publishing features, including per-licensee takedowns, collaboration-artist publishing, product overrides and a DDEX ERN 4.3 upgrade with acknowledgement tracking.
  • Built artist-management APIs (merge, associations, parallel relinking) and a bulk OpenSearch backfill; added a golden-file test framework for AWS Glue (PySpark) export jobs in CI.
